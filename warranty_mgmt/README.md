# Warranty Management for Odoo 19

Automatically create warranty coverage when eligible customer invoices and vendor bills are posted. Set automatic creation by policy, override it per product, and track lots/serials, claims, expiry reminders, and purchase traceability in the Warranty Management app.

## Requirements

- Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise
- Accounting and Purchase applications

## Main workflows

1. Assign warranty users and managers under **Settings → Users & Companies → Users**.
2. Create warranty types and policies in the Warranty app. Turn on **Automatic Creation** on policies you want to use when a source document posts.
3. On a product, enable **Warranty Available** and choose **Warranty Registration**: **Use Policy Setting**, **Manual**, or **Automatic**. Manual or Automatic on the product overrides the selected policy.
4. On posting a customer invoice or vendor bill, eligible automatic coverage is created. The source date, current date, or completed delivery/receipt date follows the selected policy's start rule.
5. Use **Give Customer Warranty** or **Register Supplier Warranty** for uncovered lines that need manual registration. Tracked items are covered per linked lot/serial; if no lot is linked, the coverage is product-level.
6. Review coverage, claims, reminders, and source totals on the Warranty dashboard.

Lines with no matching policy, an unavailable start date, or duplicate coverage are skipped and explained in document chatter. Manual, unavailable receipt, and unavailable QC dates are not guessed. The dashboard follows the user's warranty access and active company rules.

## Access levels

- **Warranty User**: manage assigned coverages and submit claims.
- **Warranty Manager**: access warranties in allowed companies and process claims.
- **Warranty Admin**: configure warranty policies and types.

History entries are generated by warranty workflows and retained as an audit trail. Coverage and claim records are retained; use cancel/void and workflow actions to close them.

## Upgrade note

`warranty_mgmt` is a new technical addon name. It does not automatically migrate databases that previously installed `smr_warranty`, `smr_warranty_account`, `smr_warranty_purchase`, or `smr_warranty_dashboard`. Back up existing databases and arrange a tested migration before switching addon names.
