# Warranty Management for Odoo 19

Warranty Management combines policy setup, product coverage, claims, expiry reminders, customer invoice registration, supplier bill registration, purchase traceability, and a unified dashboard in one Odoo app.

## Requirements

- Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise
- Accounting and Purchase applications

## Main workflows

1. Assign warranty users and managers under **Settings → Users & Companies → Users**.
2. Create warranty types and policies in the Warranty app.
3. On a posted customer invoice, use **Give Customer Warranty** to register the warranty for sold goods.
4. On a posted vendor bill, use **Register Supplier Warranty** to record supplier coverage. Vendor bill warranties retain their purchase order and purchase line where available.
5. Review coverage, claims, reminders, and source totals on the Warranty dashboard.

Each invoice or bill wizard lets the user select a policy and adjust the coverage dates for that document without changing the policy itself. The dashboard follows the user's warranty access and active company rules.

## Access levels

- **Warranty User**: manage assigned coverages and submit claims.
- **Warranty Manager**: access warranties in allowed companies and process claims.
- **Warranty Admin**: configure warranty policies and types.

History entries are generated by warranty workflows and retained as an audit trail. Coverage and claim records are retained; use cancel/void and workflow actions to close them.

## Upgrade note

`warranty_mgmt` is a new technical addon name. It does not automatically migrate databases that previously installed `smr_warranty`, `smr_warranty_account`, `smr_warranty_purchase`, or `smr_warranty_dashboard`. Back up existing databases and arrange a tested migration before switching addon names.
