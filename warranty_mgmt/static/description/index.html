Warranty Management

Automatically create warranties from customer invoices and vendor bills

Choose a default on each policy and override automatic or manual registration per product. Warranty coverage is created when eligible customer invoices and vendor bills are posted, with lot/serial traceability, claims, expiry reminders, and an Odoo-native dashboard.

Automatic warranty creation

Post a customer invoice or vendor bill to create eligible coverage from the configured policy and product settings.

Lot and serial traceability

Attach coverage to a specific lot or serial and retain source document references.

Manual control and claims

Keep selected products manual, register uncovered lines when needed, and process claims through role-based workflows.

Set the right level of automation

Choose Automatic Creation on a policy, then set products to Use Policy Setting, Manual, or Automatic. Automatic creation follows the policy date rule, covers linked lots/serials individually, and reports skipped lines in document chatter. Manual registration actions remain available for uncovered products.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise on Odoo.sh or an on-premise server. Accounting, Purchase, and Inventory are required.

Third-party Python addons are not supported on Odoo Online.
