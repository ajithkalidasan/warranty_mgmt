Warranty Management

Coverage, claims, customer invoices and supplier bills in one app

Manage warranty policies and product coverage, register customer warranties from posted invoices, record supplier warranties from vendor bills, follow purchase-order traceability, process claims, and review expiry activity in one Odoo-native dashboard.

Invoice and bill coverage

Register customer warranties from posted invoices and supplier warranties from posted vendor bills.

Lot and serial traceability

Attach coverage to a specific lot or serial and retain source document references.

Claims and expiry

Process claims through role-based workflows and review reminders and coverage status in the dashboard.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise on Odoo.sh or an on-premise server. Accounting, Purchase, and Inventory are required.

Third-party Python addons are not supported on Odoo Online.
